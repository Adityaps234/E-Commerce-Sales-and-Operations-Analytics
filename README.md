# E-Commerce Sales & Operations Analytics
### Project Overview
This project involves a comprehensive 5-page interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing over 100,000 rows of e-commerce transactional data. The objective was to track end-to-end business performance, from front-end sales and customer acquisition to back-end logistics and payment gateway success rates.
### Core Business Metrics Tracked
- **Financials**: Total Revenue (₹395.16M), Average Order Value (₹3.95K)
- **Customer Volume**: 100K Total Orders, 30K Total Customers
- **Operations & Logistics**: Payment Success Rate (82.81%), On-Time Delivery (95.13%)
### Key Business Insights Discovered
1. **Seasonal Revenue Spikes**: Sales volume shows massive peaks during January and October 2025, indicating highly successful seasonal or holiday promotional campaigns.
2. **Category Performance**: "Furniture" and "Mobile & Accessories" are the undisputed top-performing product categories, driving the vast majority of the ₹395M total revenue.
3. **Fulfillment & Payment Health**: The business maintains a strong operational flow, with 83.11% of all orders successfully delivered and an 82.81% payment gateway success rate.
